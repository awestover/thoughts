We (humanity) are in the process of building superintelligent AI. AIs with superhuman ability to: strategize, make money, code/hack, perform technology research, manipulate humans, and improve themselves. If we create such AIs, as we are on track to do, and these AIs have goals at odds with human well-being, then we will die, as surely as an elephant would die if humans decided they wanted its tusks. 

We do not know how to reliably instill desired goals into an AI, and we do not have goals that we would feel comfortable with an AI optimizing for. We do not have methods for detecting or correcting misalignment. We are developing AI in a "race", which incentivizes dismissing safety concerns. 

This is not a good situation. By default, we will lose control to AI, which most likely looks like us being dead -- you, me, your roommate and your first child.

Is there a [path](narrowpath.co) to victory? Perhaps -- but it will not be a comfortable one. 

What is this path? We need the government to push the breaks on frontier AI development until we have reason to believe that it is safe to proceed. We need international coordination to enforce this slow-down, and to work towards a stable long-term solution. To accomplish this, policy makers need to know that this issue is important to the population.

Thus, for you the few two steps along this path are:
(1) Research the risks yourself. For instance, read [this](), or [this]().
(2) Discuss the risks. You could start by sharing this post.
