Especially when busy / mentally "at capacity" I think it's really helpful to
have some good default behaviors. 

Here are a couple of low-effort thoughts on defaults that I could chose which
would improve my life:

- I'd like to have some fun traditions. Such as:
- Having high quality 1 question per week that I try to get ppl to answer. 
	- E.g., "What's your favorite piece of writing?"
	- What are some good ways that you've met friends in Berkley?
	- What's the most interesting question you've been asked lately? Of all time?
- Walking around
	- I'd originally thought that I might want to have a zumba tradition, but I'm always too exhausted for this. However I think I could enjoy walking.
- Have 1 personal goal per week. 
	- These are allowed to be very simple like "go walk around"
	- or "cook something"
	- or "buy rice pudding"
	- or "don't waste time on technology" (probably more specific operationalization is better)
- I'd like to have the following default outside of work activities: 
	- Walk around
	- Talk to ppl (or write to ppl)
	- Sleep
- I'd like to have a strong default of not just consuming stuff online
	- I suspect that eventually there's a balance, there is some really good stuff online that I'd like to read at some point, but in terms of defaults, it feels like avoiding this stuff is a safer default.
	- Here's one way to make this sound more fun: 
		- If you can't even avoid human-generated content, then you have **no hope** against AI generated content optimized for engagement.
