- Being responsible means that you don't give excuses. *You* just either get things done, or you don't. 
- There is no one else productive to blame beyond yourself. 
- The bystander effect is the antithesis of responsibility.

It is often extremely alluring to hope that someone else will do something about an important problem that would be uncomfortable to solve yourself. But it isn't necessarily [[accept the truth|true]].

When you feel like shirking responsibility, one good thing to think is "suppose that everyone thought like this; then no one would do anything about {important issue}".
