> an anti-recommendation:

avoid "meetings" -- things with large numbers of ppl (e.g., like lectures)
maximum good group size is generally 2-3, unless it's in a space where it's easy to split into smaller groups. 
for example, going on a hike with 10 ppl is still great because this will naturally split into 3-4 smaller groups.
